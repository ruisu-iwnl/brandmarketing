# Joulery by Li'L Caca

**A smart online store for handcrafted jewelry.**

Joulery is the official online store for Li'L Caca, a brand that creates beautiful handcrafted jewelry pieces. The site is built to feel clean, calm, and premium. Think of it like browsing a fashion magazine, but everything you see is something you can actually buy.


## What Customers Can Do

Customers can browse through the full jewelry collection and click on any piece to see bigger photos, a description, the price, and more details. If they love something, they can share it instantly with friends through WhatsApp, Facebook, Pinterest, X, or Reddit with just one tap.


## What the Store Owner Can Do

The store owner gets a private dashboard to manage everything without touching any code. From there, they can add new products, upload photos, update prices, and write descriptions. They can also see real information about how the store is doing, like which products people are looking at, how many have been sold, and what customers think of each piece.


## How It Tracks Performance

The store quietly keeps track of a few useful numbers in the background. Every time someone opens a product, it gets counted as a view. The total money earned per product is figured out automatically based on the price and how many were sold. The system also shows the total value of the remaining stock, the percentage of viewers who actually made a purchase, and the average star rating based on customer reviews.

All of these numbers update on their own. The owner never has to calculate or update them manually.
